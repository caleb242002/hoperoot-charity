Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible? +

Yes. HopeRoot is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. You will receive a tax receipt via email immediately after your donation is processed.

Can I cancel my monthly donation? +

Absolutely. You can cancel or modify your monthly donation at any time from your donor dashboard or by emailing us at support@hoperoot.org.

How do I know my money reaches the field? +

We publish quarterly impact reports with photos, GPS coordinates of project sites, and financial breakdowns. 98% of every donation goes directly to our programs.

Do you accept corporate partnerships? +

Yes. We work with companies of all sizes for matching gift programs, CSR initiatives, and employee volunteering. Contact partnerships@hoperoot.org.
